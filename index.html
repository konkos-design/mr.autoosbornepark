What we do

Workshop services

Tell us what the car is doing and we'll find the cause. Pick a service to start a booking.

General servicing

Oil and filter changes and scheduled servicing to keep your car running reliably.

Brakes

Brake checks, pad and rotor replacement. Ask us if the pedal feels soft or you hear grinding.

Pre-purchase inspection

Buying a used car? We check it over before you hand over the money.

Diagnostics

Warning lights, knocks and rattles. We track down faults that are hard to find.

Engine & cooling

Water pumps, belts and thermostats. Overheating sorted before it becomes engine damage.

Transmission

Transmission fluid services and checks for slipping or harsh gear changes.

Suspension & steering

Clunks over bumps, pulling to one side or uneven tyre wear.

Electrical & air-con

Electrical faults and air-conditioning checks, ready for a Perth summer.
